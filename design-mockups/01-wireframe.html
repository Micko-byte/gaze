Motion & technical notes baked in

  • Smooth scroll — Lenis with rubber-band easing. Reduced-motion respected throughout.
  • Hero video — lazy-loaded MP4 + WebM, max 4MB, autoplay muted playsinline. Mobile = high-quality still poster, video swap on desktop + good connection only.
  • Custom cursor — thin rose-gold ring on desktop, scales 1.5× on interactive elements, hidden on touch devices.
  • Section transitions — SplitType letter reveal on headlines, scroll-pinned scenes for Ethos and Synergy, masked clip-path reveals at section boundaries.
  • Route transitions — black curtain wipe with a centred rose mark for /institute and /manor.
  • Mobile-first — per strategy doc, 80% of traffic. Horizontal division scroll becomes stacked vertical reveals on mobile. Hero loses video for poster + animated atmosphere fallback.
  • Form routing — Resend for transactional email; division dropdown tags submissions; CRM webhook to HubSpot or ActiveCampaign for nurture (later, not in this scaffold).
  • Stack — Next.js 14 App Router + TypeScript + Tailwind + GSAP + Lenis + Framer Motion. Content hardcoded in typed TS constants; Sanity wired in a follow-up.
  • SEO — JSON-LD Organization schema, per-section Open Graph imagery, sitemap, robots, canonical.
