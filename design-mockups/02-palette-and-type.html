Gaze Holdings — Brand Direction · v2 (Logo-led)

Refined for the logo & ultra-luxury read.

Pivoting away from heritage gold-and-obsidian. New direction draws from the existing Gaze Furnishings logo — dusty rose accent, obsidian black, geometric sans — positioned at the editorial end of luxury (closer to Bottega Veneta / The Row / Le Labo than Aman Resorts).

Obsidian dominates as the canvas. Ivory carries body copy on dark backgrounds. House Rose — pulled directly from the Furnishings logo — is the single saturated accent: used sparingly for italics, division markers, and the gold-ring cursor. Champagne is the quiet metallic for hairlines and division dividers, replacing the original brief's gold without competing with rose.

Old direction (brief)

Heritage gold-and-bronze. Reads as men's-club / private-bank / Aman. No connection to existing logo.

New direction (logo-led)

Editorial-feminine luxury. Inherits the Furnishings logo. Reads as Bottega / The Row / Jacquemus editorial. Aligns with HerGaze (female-led) without becoming overtly feminine.

Why this direction

Outfit picks up the geometric sans character of the existing GAZE wordmark. Italic Cormorant on accent words ("legacy", "vision") gives the editorial luxury moment. Inter handles all body. Cohesive with the logo. Most "Bottega" of the three.

Why this direction

Pure editorial serif display. Treats the website as a counterpoint to the logo (sans logo, serif site — like luxury hospitality). Maximum heritage feel. Slightly less coherent with the existing visual identity but more "ultra-luxury" in the classical sense.

Manrope all-caps tracked wide for headlines — reads as the quietest, most refined sans. Italic serif accent breaks the sans rhythm. Most "fashion luxury" of the three. Strongest for younger high-net-worth aesthetic. Slightly colder.
