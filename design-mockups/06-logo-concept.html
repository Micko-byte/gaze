What changed from previous versions: The triangle and decorative lines are gone. The only visual "action" is the two italic G's facing each other — their natural lean toward the centre does the work. The mark is fully monochrome (inherits text colour) so it lives on obsidian, ivory, or any future background without touching the site's palette.

Colour flexibility: Wrapping the mark in a coloured element changes it instantly — rose, champagne, or white-on-dark all work. The rose separator dot in the inline variant is the only hardcoded accent and can be removed or swapped in one line.

Font: Cormorant Garamond 300 italic for the G's (already in the design system); Outfit 300 for the wordmark. Georgia renders as fallback until Cormorant is confirmed.
