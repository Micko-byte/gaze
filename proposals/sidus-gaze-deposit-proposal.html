Scope

This KSh 100,000 covers Phase 1 — the Gaze Holdings Hub plus the core integrations. The four microsites (Furnishings store, Press Global, HerGaze, and the Institute / Manor build-out) are later phases with their own budgets.

Stack advantage — PayOnTime is in-house: no third-party CRM SaaS fee (HubSpot / ActiveCampaign would run KSh 3,000–15,000 per month) and no payment-gateway markup beyond standard M-Pesa / card processing. More of the budget goes to the build.
