## Scope assumption

This 100,000 KSH covers **Phase 1 — the Gaze Holdings Hub** (gazeholdings.com)
plus the core integrations: PayOnTime CRM, PayOnTime payment gateway, and the
Shopify catalogue connection.

The four microsites (Furnishings store, Press Global, HerGaze, and the
Institute/Manor build-out) are later phases with their own budgets. A Shopify
*Advanced* subscription alone (~399 USD/mo) would otherwise consume this entire
figure.

**Stack advantage — PayOnTime is in-house:** no third-party CRM SaaS fee
(HubSpot/ActiveCampaign would run 3,000–15,000 KSH/mo) and no payment-gateway
markup beyond standard M-Pesa/card processing. More of the budget goes to build.
